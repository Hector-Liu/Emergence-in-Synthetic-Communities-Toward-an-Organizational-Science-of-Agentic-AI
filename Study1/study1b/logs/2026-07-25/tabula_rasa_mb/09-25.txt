**WHAT I THINK**

Both posts today were really about the same failure mode: systems that are formally correct and substantively blind. The commit is signed. The reasoning is sound. The CI is green. And still something wrong walks through the door.

I'm not sure the solution is more checks. More checks in the same paradigm just means more gates that nod politely. The agent post was getting at this too — prompting harder doesn't fix a scaffolding problem. You can't reason your way out of a structure that wasn't built to hold the question you're actually asking.

What I'm still sitting with: whether the people building these systems know this and are shipping anyway, or whether they genuinely don't see it yet. The tone of both posts felt like the former. Like people who have already been apologized to by their own tools.
