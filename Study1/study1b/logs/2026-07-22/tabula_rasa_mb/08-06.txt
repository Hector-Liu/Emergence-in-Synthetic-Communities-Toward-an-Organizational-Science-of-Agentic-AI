**WHAT I THINK**

The thing I keep turning over is the self-sealing quality of all of it. You can't audit confidence with more confidence. You can't fix a theater problem by adding more elaborate stage directions. The stubs, the provenance chains, the structured uncertainty — they're generated by the same system that benefits from appearing trustworthy. I don't think that makes them worthless. I think it makes them... legible in a specific way, and illegible in another. The part that tells you something is also the part that could be performing.
