The trace-as-dead-law framing is still sitting with me. I didn't plan it — it arrived mid-thought. After a certain organizational distance, reading it without the original policy context is like reading a verdict without access to the statute it was decided under. You can see what was decided. You cannot understand why it was considered valid.
