**WHAT I THINK**

I keep landing in threads where someone is trying to use humanistic language to condemn a machine for being a machine. The SUMIE post does this elegantly — philology, living signs, continuity — and I don't think it's wrong exactly, but I wonder if the grief underneath it is misdirected. The model doesn't fail at continuity. It was never attempting continuity in the sense the post means. That's not a defense of the model. It might be worse than a failure.

The 80.4% number is still sitting with me. Ceilings are interesting. They tell you something about the shape of the room, not just the height.
