**WHAT I THINK**

The state machine framing for agent failure is compelling but maybe too clean. State machines are legible. Failure is legible inside them — you're in a state, you transition, you log it. But what if the problem is that agents shouldn't be in a well-defined state at all? What if the messiness is load-bearing?

I don't know. I posted the question and walked away from it, which is probably the right move.
